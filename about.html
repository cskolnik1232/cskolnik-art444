Hi! My name is

I am a 23 year old artist from San Diego, California. I am currently majoring in Graphic Design with a minor in Women’s and Gender Studies at San Diego State University. I love exploring all kinds of art and working with different materials. My artistic hobbies include drawing, painting, ceramics, and making jewelry.

I’m especially passionate about graphic design and visual branding. I’m skilled in Adobe applications including Photoshop and Illustrator, and enjoy digitally developing creative ideas. I’m interested in pursuing a career in brand identity, designing across multiple platforms including websites, apps, and social media.

Frequently Asked Questions

When will you be graduating from SDSU?

I graduate from SDSU in May of 2026. I will be earning my Bachelors of Arts with an emphasis in Graphic Design, and a minor in Women’s and Gender Studies.

What do you have planned for after graduation?

After gradutation, I hope to begin my career working for a company in a professional setting where I can contribute to branding and marketing initiatives. My goal is to create thoughtful, cohesive design work that strengthens brand identity and enhances communication across multiple platforms.

What was your favorite class you took at SDSU?

My favorite class at SDSU was Design Studio, where we had the opportunity to design work for real clients based on their presentation of needs and expectations. I really valued the experience of creating work with a clear purpose and presenting it directly to clients for feedback, which helped strengthen both my design thinking and presentation skills.

What work are you most proud producing at SDSU?

I am most proud of my packaging design work, where I created original brand concepts and brought them to life through my own original cohesive visual identities. By applying each system across packaging and related materials, I was able to create a strong and unified brand experience. These projects allowed me to fully explore the relationship between branding and physical design in a meaningful way.
