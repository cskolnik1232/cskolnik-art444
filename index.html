PROTOCOL GRIP TRAINER

design studio - 2025

This project focused on developing a brand identity for a client’s new product, the Protocol Grip Trainer. The creator presented his vision to the design studio class, and I developed my own interpretation of the brand. The goal was to create a strong, cohesive identity that reflected a modern fitness aesthetic, using cool blue tones to align with the product’s look and appeal to a performance-driven audience. I began by designing a logo and establishing a consistent visual system,

including typography, color palette, and graphic elements. This system was then applied across multiple media types, including product packaging, a responsive website, a companion app interface, and various marketing materials such as advertisements and tabling assets. The final result is a cohesive and visually engaging brand experience that translates seamlessly across physical and digital platforms. My design was ultimately selected to be implemented for the client’s product.

SCCUR POSTER SERIES

design studio - october 2025

This project focused on creating a series of marketing posters for the SDSU Student Symposium. The event organizers presented the concept and goals to the design studio, and I developed my own visual interpretation. Centered around the theme “Upholding Discovery,” the project emphasized innovation, learning, and the pursuit of future ideas. I translated this concept into a bold visual direction using a green neon space-inspired theme, symbolizing exploration, curiosity, and forward-thinking. To support the theme of discovery across disciplines,

I created a series of custom symbols representing different fields of study. These elements were integrated into the compositions to highlight the inclusion of all majors and the broad spectrum of exploration represented at the symposium. I combined these icons with high-contrast color, glowing neon effects, and layered layouts to create depth and visual interest. The final poster series captures a futuristic tone while clearly communicating the event’s message of innovation and cross-disciplinary discovery.

DATA SELFIE POSTER SERIES

graphic design 3 - april 2025

This project, titled Data Selfie Poster Series, is a personal exploration that visualizes data from my everyday life. The series reflects different aspects of my identity, including listening habits, reading patterns, and creative work, through thoughtfully organized and stylized data. The goal was to transform personal information into engaging visual compositions, using design to tell a story about who I am through measurable patterns and habits.

I developed a cohesive visual system across the three posters, using color, typography, and layout to differentiate each theme while maintaining a unified style. Each poster incorporates custom data visualizations, including charts, icons, and structured layouts, to present information in a clear and visually engaging way. Soft gradients, bold type, and graphic elements were used to create a balance between readability and aesthetic appeal.

INFOGRAPHIC POSTER AND INTERACTIVE DESIGN

graphic design 3 - february 2025

This project focused on the creation of an informational infographic and an interactive companion map centered on endangered frog species. The goal was to raise awareness about the environmental threats impacting amphibians, including habitat destruction, climate change, pollution, and disease. By combining data visualization with engaging visuals, the project aims to educate viewers while encouraging a deeper understanding of biodiversity loss on a global scale. I designed a cohesive visual system that extends

across both the infographic and the interactive map, using a natural, earthy color palette and illustrative elements to reflect the subject matter. The infographic presents key data through charts, icons, and structured layouts, while the companion map allows users to explore the geographic distribution of endangered species in a more engaging way. Custom illustrations of frogs and simplified visual markers were used to make the information approachable and visually clear.

TRINKET TRADER APP DESIGN

ui/ux design - march 2025

This project focused on the design of Trinket Trader, a mobile app created in collaboration with Julia Sena. The app is designed as a marketplace for collectors, allowing users to buy, sell, and trade collectible items while also keeping track of what they own and what they are looking for. The goal was to create a user-friendly and engaging platform that simplifies the trading process while fostering a sense of community among collectors. We developed a cohesive and playful visual identity using soft colors, approachable illustrations,

and intuitive navigation to make the experience feel welcoming and easy to use. Key features include browsing and searching for items, organizing personal collections, managing trades, and tracking desired items. The interface was designed to balance functionality with personality, incorporating custom graphics and clear layouts to enhance usability. The final design creates a seamless and enjoyable user experience that supports both casual browsing and active trading within the collector community.

UI OF THE WEEK CHALLENGE

ui/ux design - february 2025

This project is a collection of designs from a User Interface of the Week challenge, where each week focused on learning and applying a new UI component or tool. The goal was to explore a range of interface elements, such as sliders, navigation bars, menus, and progress indicators, by designing original app screens that incorporate each feature. Each prompt allowed for creative freedom in concept, resulting in a variety of app ideas across different categories. For each design, I focused on creating intuitive layouts that clearly demonstrate

the functionality of the assigned UI element while maintaining a cohesive and visually engaging interface. I experimented with different styles, color palettes, and interaction patterns to adapt each component to its specific context, from finance and fitness apps to food ordering and file management. This series strengthened my understanding of usability, hierarchy, and consistency across interfaces, resulting in a diverse set of polished screens that highlight both technical skills and creative problem-solving in UI design.
